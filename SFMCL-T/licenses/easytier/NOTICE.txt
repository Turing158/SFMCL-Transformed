Source (exact revision): https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/tree/8428a89d2dabc94c97d370ec607c6ca142473626
Corresponding source archive: https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/archive/refs/tags/v2.6.4.tar.gz
Official binaries: https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/releases/tag/v2.6.4
No modifications are made to the upstream executables. SFMCL-T generates configuration
in memory and communicates through stdin and the local EasyTier CLI/RPC interface.
easytier-core.exe and easytier-cli.exe are downloaded from upstream on demand.
The archive's Npcap Packet.dll is NOT installed or distributed by this project.
The required Packet.dll import is supplied by the BSD-licensed WinPcap 4.1.3
compatibility library (see WINPCAP-LICENSE). No driver is installed. Packet
capture is not enabled; the application exclusively uses the no-TUN proxy mode.
WinPcap binaries originate from https://www.winpcap.org/install/bin/WinPcap_4_1_3.exe
